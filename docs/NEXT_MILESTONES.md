# Development plan after the foundation

## 1. Experience validation

- Complete hands-on browser, keyboard, assistive-technology, touch, 200% zoom, and device/GPU testing.
- Evaluate uploaded face framing across image sizes, angles, lighting, skin tones, and facial geometry; replace fixed framing with an evaluated capture pipeline before precision claims.
- Test identity preservation and prevent unrealistic visual changes from being interpreted as achievable treatment outcomes.
- Review copy and accessibility with consumers and qualified clinicians.

## 2. Connected clinic pilot

- Select launch jurisdictions and confirm lawful practitioner referral arrangements.
- Add verified clinics, practitioners, organization membership, server-side roles, and patient-specific sharing grants.
- Add real scheduling-provider integration with idempotent appointment creation, cancellation, and authoritative booking status.
- Implement distinct permission records for storing, sharing, follow-up, marketing, and research. Enable opt-out and revocation.
- Add operational retention schedules, export/deletion workflows, audit trails, backup restoration tests, monitoring, rate limits, and incident response.
- Verify clinic domains and iframe policies. The current website-origin field is planning configuration, not an enforced public domain allowlist.
- Test conversion claims with consent-aware funnel analytics and controlled experiments. Do not optimize by encouraging unnecessary treatment.

## 3. Validated treatment response

- Recruit clinical leadership and establish intended use and regulatory classification.
- Collect standardized longitudinal observations with treatment product, region, clinician-confirmed inputs, and assessment-quality capture.
- Develop separate product- and tissue-specific models. Botox products' units and filler products must not be treated as universally interchangeable quantities.
- Model onset, peak, wear-off, variability, prior treatment, asymmetric responses, and contraindicated/out-of-distribution cases.
- Define acceptance criteria before training: calibration, uncertainty coverage, appearance realism, demographic subgroup performance, abstention, reproducibility, and clinical relevance.
- Run prospective external validation before advertising patient-specific predictive performance.

## 4. Clinician-only planning assistance

- Require confirmed clinical assessment and qualified-user authorization.
- Validate candidate target rationale, alternatives, uncertainty, and human oversight separately from visual rendering.
- Complete human-factors testing, risk management, change control, and post-release monitoring before enabling any target or dose assistance.
- Keep autonomous prescribing and self-injection instructions out of the consumer product.

## 5. Scale and differentiation

- Benchmark against named current competitors using documented criteria and independently reproducible evidence.
- Track expectation alignment, patient-reported understanding, decision quality, clinician usefulness, calibrated prediction error, and consultation completion.
- Treat reduced errors, increased conversions, and superior accuracy as hypotheses until measured.
- Maintain a model registry, versioned simulation record, consent provenance, monitoring, rollback, and release governance.

This plan implements the staged intent of the approved comprehensive PRD. The original private planning documents remain in the user's project deliverables and have not been placed into the public source repository.
